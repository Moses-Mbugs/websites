The process

Three steps between browsing and holding the keys.

No agents to negotiate through. The clock and the bid ledger do that work in public.

01 / Register

Verify once

A short identity and funds check unlocks bidding across every lot on Provenance — no repeat paperwork per house.

02 / Bid

Bid in the open

Every bid posts instantly to the public ledger for that lot. You always see the current price and how many are competing.

03 / Close

Clock hits zero

The highest bid when the countdown ends wins the lot. Funds settle in escrow within 5 business days.
